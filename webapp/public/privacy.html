Squook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Squook makes explainer and lesson videos. It is run by Saquib Jawed, Ranchi, India. Contact: saquibjawed4444@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your email address when you sign in, the text, links, photos and videos you give us to make a video, and the videos we make for you. Share links record a view count and time.

YouTube and Google data

If you connect a YouTube channel, Squook uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the videos you made with Squook and set their title, description and thumbnail, and the YouTube Analytics API only to read view counts for those videos. We store the access token privately and never sell it or share it. Squook's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Squook uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Squook's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How we use it

Only to make, store, share and publish your videos and to support you. We do not sell personal data or use it for ads.

Services we use

Cloudflare (hosting), Supabase (sign-in and storage), and AI, voice and stock-media providers that receive only the content needed to make your video.

Deleting your data

Email saquibjawed4444@gmail.com and we will delete your account, videos and any stored tokens within 7 days.
